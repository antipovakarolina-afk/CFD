Important risk notice

CFDs are complex, leveraged products and carry a high risk of losing money rapidly. They may not be suitable for all retail investors. Leverage magnifies losses as well as gains. You can lose money even when you understand the mechanics of the product.

All examples on this site are hypothetical and simplified. They are not live prices, financial advice, investment research or recommendations to buy, sell, hold or short any instrument. They exclude costs unless stated otherwise.

Before considering a CFD, read the provider's key information document, risk warning, terms, contract specifications, fee schedule and order execution policy. Check whether the provider is authorised by the relevant regulator. In Ireland, consult current information from the Central Bank of Ireland; EU-level product intervention and investor information may also be available from ESMA. Rules and protections can change.

Never use money needed for essential living costs, and do not borrow to speculate. Consider whether you understand the product and can afford the risk. This course does not assess suitability.
